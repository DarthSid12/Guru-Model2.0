# Evidence for the bin5 training draft

Run: `runs/faces_vgg2k_objects_houses_zubud137_41_houses_lp_16fix_lr0.001_vgg16_bn_aa5_r21vgg2k_vgg16_bn_aa5_s42`.

The draft follows the supplied training / Data / Transformations structure. It excludes the behavioral experiments and their held-out stimulus sets.

- `config.json` identifies `vgg16_bn_aa5`, scratch initialization, temperature 2, dropout 0.3, AdamW settings, augmentation, sampling, and the full curriculum. Its top-level `epochs: 50` is the parser value saved before curriculum construction. `train.py` replaces it with the sum of stage durations. `summary.json` and all 124 history rows confirm the actual duration of 124 epochs.
- `model.py` defines the VGG convolutional extractor, global average pooling, 512-to-256 projection, continuous sigmoid code, dropout, and classifier. `model.stochastic=False` means no Bernoulli sampling during training. Calling this a deterministically sampled binary code would be incorrect.
- Installed `antialiased_cnns` source (`vgg.py` and `blurpool.py`, version pinned to 0.3 in `requirements.txt`) confirms five max-pool/BlurPool stages, stride-one max pooling, stride-two fixed filtering, binomial coefficients `[1,4,6,4,1]`, and reflection padding. Network convolution layers retain their ordinary zero padding.
- `train.py` implements square-root sampling across four separate groups, square-root weighting by photos within a group, active-class masking, the global cosine schedule, warm-up after stages 1–9, 2,000 steps per epoch, and stage-local stopping counters.
- `summary.json` and the history identify epoch 122 as best validation and epoch 124 as final. Current `kanwisher_tests/run_latest_vgg_kanwisher.py` and the latest bin5 Yin manifests refer to `final_model_20260927_145006.pth`. The draft records both checkpoints and does not claim that later analyses use the best checkpoint.
- Packed `meta.json` files provide the image and class counts. Training face counts are 200 for 2,047 identities and 181 for one identity, totaling 409,581. Raw dataset length reported in the run summary counts fixation slots, not unique images, and includes the generic-house pool before curriculum caps.
- `data_preparation/make_faces_vgg2k.py` and `data/vggface2_raw/faces_vgg2k_order_race.csv` establish the identity order and operational demographic grouping. Stage 9 contains 823 Caucasian, 118 African, 74 Asian, and 9 Indian identities; stage 10 contains 1,500, 319, 207, and 22, respectively. These are classifier/manual assignments, not self-reported demographics. The construction merges FairFace White and Middle Eastern predictions, and East Asian and Southeast Asian predictions; ambiguous candidates are excluded by the script's probability thresholds.
- `data_preparation/make_houses_41.py` confirms four training views and one validation view per building. There is no separate test split for these 137 buildings. `download.py` establishes the generic house source and the 435-image training pool. Its 100 validation and 100 test entries represent the same source photographs, so the draft does not present them as independent sets.
- `salience_trans.py`, `trans.py`, `datasets.py`, and `preprocess_fixations.py` establish the preprocessing and fixation details. The current augmentation applies crop/scale/position variation and per-sample rotation; color jitter, erasing, and flips are absent. Sampling with replacement means the 16 fixation slots should not be described as exactly 16 distinct views of every image per epoch.
- The fourth Gabor orientation is `3*pi/4`; the supplied old text's `3*pi/2` was incorrect. The center prior is the square of a Gaussian with sigma `W/4`.
- The log-polar transform's default `smoothing=0` gives equal weights to four neighboring samples; it is not conventional bilinear interpolation. Boundary indices are clamped. The draft reserves the term bilinear for random resized crops, where it is accurate.

(2018)](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/publications/2018/Cao18/cao18.pdf). Existing Bengio, Yamada, and Jiang citation keys were carried forward from the supplied text; their bibliography entries were not available here. A FairFace citation should also be added when matching the manuscript bibliography.
